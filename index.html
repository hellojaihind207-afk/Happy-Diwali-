THE STORY

Why we light the diya

Diwali, the festival of lights, celebrates the win of light over darkness and good over evil. Every region tells it a little differently.

Rama comes home

After 14 years in exile, Rama, Sita and Lakshmana returned to Ayodhya. The people lit rows of lamps to welcome them.

Goddess Lakshmi

Homes are cleaned and decorated so the Goddess of wealth and wellbeing is welcomed on Diwali night.

Light over darkness

Naraka Chaturdashi remembers Krishna defeating the demon Narakasura. In South India this is the main Deepavali morning.
